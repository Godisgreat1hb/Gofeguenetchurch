What We Do

Youth Activities & Ministries

Discover our diverse programs designed to build your faith, sharpen your talents, and foster Christian fellowship.

1. Prayer and Worship

Deepening our intimacy with God through fervent weekly prayer sessions, tarrying nights, and spirit-filled worship.

2. Bible Study

Systematic study of the Holy Scriptures to build strong doctrinal foundations and practical life wisdom for youth.

3. Youth Fellowship

Dynamic Saturday gatherings featuring testimonies, Christian Q&A panels, godly discussions, and heartwarming socialization.

4. Gospel Outreach

Taking the Good News of Jesus Christ to neighborhoods, schools, campuses, and surrounding towns through evangelism.

5. Community Service

Demonstrating Christ’s love in action by supporting vulnerable families, cleaning neighborhoods, and charity drives.

6. Choir and Music

Training talented youth voices and instrumentalists to lead the congregation into profound worship during Sunday services.

7. Youth Leadership

Mentoring upcoming leaders with integrity, godly character, administrative skills, and stewardship principles.

8. Educational Support

Tutorial programs, career guidance, and academic mentorship to help students excel in school and university.

9. Sports and Recreation

Fostering teamwork, physical fitness, and healthy friendships through football tournaments, indoor games, and picnics.

10. Spiritual Development

Discipleship classes, baptism counseling, and spiritual retreats designed for lifelong devotion to Jesus Christ.
